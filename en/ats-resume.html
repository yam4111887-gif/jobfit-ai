The ATS-Friendly Resume Guide (2026)

Your beautifully designed two-column resume may look, to an applicant tracking system, like a pile of scrambled text. Here's how ATS software actually reads resumes, which mistakes to avoid, and how to check yours for free.

What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is the software companies use to manage hiring — Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, and dozens more. When you apply, the system parses your resume into structured fields (name, education, each role's title and dates) so recruiters can search, rank, and filter. With hundreds of applicants per opening, the ATS is the first gate your resume has to pass.

The 5 most common ATS mistakes

  1. Tables and multi-column layouts: parsers read left to right, line by line — a two-column design interleaves your skills into your experience.
  2. Text inside images or text boxes: your name rendered as a logo image is invisible to the parser.
  3. Creative section headings: "Where I've Made Magic" instead of "Experience" breaks section detection. Use standard headings.
  4. Decorative bullets and exotic fonts: unusual glyphs can turn into garbage characters. Plain "-" or "•" is fine.
  5. Keyword mismatch: the post says "SEO"; you wrote "search engine optimization". Put both forms in — if you actually have the skill.

ATS-friendly checklist

  • Single column, top to bottom
  • Standard headings: Education, Experience, Skills
  • Plain-text contact info at the top
  • No tables, no text-in-images, no text boxes
  • Save as text-based PDF (not a scan) or .docx
  • At least 3 quantified achievements
  • Job-post keyword coverage mapped onto real experience

An honest note: format is table stakes, not the game

Many paid ATS checkers sell anxiety. The data-backed reality: most resumes are rejected for missing qualifications and keywords, not formatting. Clear the format landmines (avoid technical misreads), then focus on what decides outcomes — content match with the job post, which our match analysis covers in depth.

FAQ

Is an ATS check really free here?

Yes — the ATS format check runs entirely in your browser, needs no account and no API key, and your resume is never uploaded anywhere.

Do small companies use ATS too?

Larger companies and tech firms almost always do; small companies may review manually — but an ATS-friendly format is also simply the clearest format for a human skimmer. There's no trade-off.
