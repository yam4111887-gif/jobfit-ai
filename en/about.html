About Us

JobFit AI is an independently developed job search tool, built in Taiwan in 2026.

Why we built this

Most job seekers' problem isn't that they aren't good enough — it's that their resume speaks a different language than the job post. You write "responsible for backend development"; the post asks for "experience with microservice architecture". You have that experience; you just never said it in their words. The resume gets filtered out in round one, and you never learn why.

What we do is simple: put the job post and your resume side by side, review you through their lens, and show you where the gaps are and how to close them.

Our principles

  • No fabrication. AI may only reorganize your real experience, never invent it. Interviews see through fabricated resumes every time.
  • No uploads. Your resume lives in your own browser; AI analysis runs through your own API key. We have no server holding anyone's data.
  • No lock-in. Whether your resume is on 104, Cake, LinkedIn, or in a Word file — copy, paste, done.
  • An honest take on ATS. Resumes get rejected mostly for missing qualifications and keywords, not formatting. We offer a free format check to reduce risk — we don't sell anxiety.

Business model

Currently completely free: AI features run on your own API key (BYOK), so we have zero server costs and can stay free forever. A Pro hosted tier (no setup, AI credits included, monthly subscription) and plans for schools and career centers are planned. The free tier stays.
