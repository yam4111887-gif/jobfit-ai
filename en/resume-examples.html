Resume format: five ground rules

  1. One page as the default: under ~10 years of experience, one page. Recruiters spend 6-7 seconds on the first pass.
  2. Standard section order: name & contact → education (first for new grads) or experience (first if you have it) → skills → projects/certs.
  3. Bullets, not paragraphs: 2-4 bullets per role, one achievement per line, starting with a verb (built, led, shipped, reduced).
  4. Numbers speak: "improved performance" loses to "cut API latency from 800ms to 200ms (-75%)".
  5. Tailor per job: sending one resume everywhere is the biggest mistake. Map the job post's keywords onto your real experience — our tool does the diff for free.

Must-have sections checklist

  • Name, email, phone
  • Education: school, major, degree, years (reverse order)
  • Experience: company, title, dates, 2-4 quantified bullets
  • Skills: grouped by category; skip "Windows, Office"
  • Optional: portfolio/GitHub links, languages, certifications

FAQ

Where can I get free resume templates?

The examples in this hub are plain-text format — copy them into Word or Google Docs and go. Plain text is also the most ATS-friendly format, unlike fancy design templates that often get misparsed.

How long should a resume be?

One page for most people; two pages only when 10+ years of directly relevant experience genuinely demands it. Every extra page halves the odds it gets read.
