Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

JobFit AI is designed around one principle: "your resume never passes through us." This policy explains how data actually flows.

What data we collect

None. This site has no accounts, no backend server, and no database. We do not collect, store, or see anything you type.

Where your data lives

  • Resume, job description, and analysis history: stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device (unless you trigger an AI task — see below). Clearing browser data deletes everything.
  • API settings (endpoint and key): also stored only in your browser's localStorage.

How data flows during AI analysis

When you click "Analyze Match", "Tailor Resume", or "Mock Interview", your job description and resume are sent directly from your browser to the API endpoint you configured (e.g., OpenAI or Z.ai). That transfer and the provider's data handling are governed by that provider's own privacy policy. The ATS format check runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies, no ad trackers, and no third-party web analytics.

External resources

This page loads the open-source markdown rendering library (marked.js) from the jsDelivr CDN. The CDN provider logs technical data such as IP for that request, under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If we ever introduce accounts or hosted services that change this policy, we will update this page with a new date.
