FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The free tier includes all features with unlimited usage — it runs on your own API key (BYOK), so we have no server costs and can stay free forever.

Do I need an API key?

The three AI features (match analysis, resume tailoring, mock interview) need one. You can get a key from OpenAI, Z.ai, DeepSeek and others — most offer free tiers. The ATS format check runs locally and needs no key.

Is my resume uploaded to your servers?

No. We have no servers. Your resume stays in your browser's localStorage; during AI analysis, content goes directly from your browser to the API endpoint you configure. See the privacy policy.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT can do this, but you must know what to ask, re-paste everything each time, and results get lost in conversations. Here it's a productized flow: a fixed analysis structure (score, strengths, gaps, keywords), reviewable history, a strict no-fabrication rewriting rule, plus a local ATS check.

How does it compare to Cake, Teal, and similar services?

Three key differences: no platform lock-in (paste any resume — you don't have to rebuild it inside our system); free and unlimited (similar services limit free uses or charge monthly); and an all-in-one flow from match to rewrite to interview prep (most services cover only one part).

Does a high ATS score guarantee passing screening systems?

Honestly, no. Resumes are rejected mostly for missing qualifications and keywords; formatting is a smaller factor. The format check removes the "misread by the parser" risk; for content-level fit, use the match analysis.

Is Chinese supported?

Yes — the tool interface and AI output support Traditional Chinese and English (toggle in the header). Chinese analysis uses a Taiwan job-market lens. This English site is new; more content is on the way.

Will the AI fabricate experience I don't have?

The rewrite feature has an explicit system-level prohibition: it may only reorganize, reword, and reorder — never add education, titles, numbers, or certificates you don't have. We still recommend reviewing every line, which is also the best interview prep.
